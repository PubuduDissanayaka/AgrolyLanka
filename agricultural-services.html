Before you commit

The questions behind the questions

I want to grow crops more efficiently and reliably, but I need the right greenhouse system, growing media, irrigation or hydroponic solution and expert guidance to get the best results. What growers tell us
Will it actually suit my crop and my climate?
That is the first thing we work out, before anything is designed or priced. We build custom systems for different climates and specify around the crop you intend to grow — which is why the conversation starts with your requirement rather than our catalogue.
How consistent is the quality going to be?
We keep quality and traceability through our coir based products, so batches behave predictably. For systems and structures, we specify the parts together so they are matched rather than assembled from whatever was available.
What is this going to cost?
It depends on the product, the design, the project size and your requirements — which is exactly why we quote instead of publishing a price list. You get a figure that reflects your project, not an average one.
Can I rely on the supplier afterwards?
We are not only selling you a structure. Consultancy, growing media, inputs and training all come from the same team, so there is someone to call who already knows the project.
Is the investment a sustainable one?
Our coconut based raw materials are sourced directly from rural, family based producers at fair prices, and the growing media is made from a by-product of the coconut harvest. The sustainability is in how the supply chain works, not just in what it is called.

Ask us anything

Not sure which part you need?

Describe what you are growing and what is not working. We will tell you which of these actually helps — and quote for it.
