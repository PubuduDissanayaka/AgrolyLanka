Greenhouse construction

Greenhouses built for the crop and the climate

Poly-tunnel systems and custom greenhouse structures — designed, constructed and supported by one team, with the irrigation, hydroponics and growing media specified to match.

What we build

Two ways to cover a crop

Both start the same way — with what you intend to grow and the conditions you are growing in. What changes is the structure that follows from it.

01

Poly-tunnel systems

Covered growing under a tunnel structure — the practical starting point for most growers moving from open field to protected cultivation.

We handle the construction, and specify the irrigation and growing media that go inside it so the parts are matched from the outset.

  • Built for protected cultivation at working scale
  • Irrigation and hydroponic systems fitted to the layout
  • Coco peat grow bags, slabs and planter bags supplied to suit
02

Custom greenhouse systems

Where the crop, the site or the climate calls for something other than a standard tunnel, we design the structure around the requirement rather than fitting the requirement to a structure.

This is where greenhouse consultancy does the most work — deciding what the building actually has to achieve before anything is priced or built.

  • Designed for the climate the structure will stand in
  • Specified around your crop and cultivation method
  • Customised solutions for particular agricultural requirements

Our approach to greenhouse design

A structure is only right if the crop, the climate and the system inside it were considered together.

How a design is decided

Four things that shape the build

Yes — we can design a greenhouse specifically for your climate and crop requirements. These are the inputs we work from.

What you intend to grow

The crop sets the brief. How it is planted, how it is supported, how often it is handled and what its roots need all feed into the structure and the growing media we recommend.

Tell us the crop first and the rest of the conversation gets much shorter.

The conditions on site

We build custom greenhouse systems for different climates. A structure that performs in one set of conditions is not automatically the right answer somewhere else.

The climate your site actually experiences is part of the design brief, not an afterthought.

Space, access and project size

The size of the project shapes both the design and the cost. That is the main reason we quote rather than publish a price list — the same crop at two different scales is two different projects.

We would rather size it honestly than sell you more structure than the operation needs.

What goes inside it

Irrigation solutions, hydroponic systems, seeds and planting materials, and coco peat based growing media — we supply these too, so they can be specified alongside the structure instead of bolted on afterwards.

Where it helps, we run training so your team can operate the finished system confidently.

Common questions

What growers ask us first

If your question is not here, send it over on WhatsApp — a short message is usually enough for us to point you in the right direction.

What types of greenhouse systems do you design and construct?

Poly-tunnel systems and custom greenhouse systems. The custom side covers structures designed for particular climates, crops and cultivation methods where a standard tunnel is not the right fit.

Can you design a greenhouse for my climate and crop?

Yes. Designing for different climates and specific crop requirements is a core part of what we do, and it is where the consultancy work starts.

What construction and consultancy services do you provide?

Greenhouse construction and poly-tunnel systems, greenhouse consultancy, and broader agricultural consultancy and advisory services. In practice most projects use more than one of these.

Do you provide irrigation and hydroponic systems?

Yes — irrigation solutions and hydroponic systems, supplied and specified alongside the greenhouse so they suit the structure and the crop.

Can you provide customised solutions for my requirements?

Yes. Customised solutions for particular agricultural requirements are part of the service, which is exactly why we start with a conversation rather than a catalogue.

How do I get a quotation for a greenhouse project?

Message us on WhatsApp or send a quotation request with the crop, the site and the scale you have in mind. We come back with tailored advice and a quotation.

Pricing depends on the product, the greenhouse design, the project size and your requirements. Warranties and guarantees depend on what is being supplied, and are set out as part of the quotation.
